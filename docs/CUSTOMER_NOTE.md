To: Head of Support

Subject: What the Paylane support agent can handle

The agent can verify a customer, handle small eligible refunds, explain pending charges, and send the right cases to a support specialist. It will not guess why a card is frozen, promise money back, bypass verification, or approve a refund outside the agreed rules.

Before launch, please confirm who owns escalated tickets and how quickly they should respond. We also need the final refund workflow and policy, the support and transaction data the team may use, and confirmation that the agent’s verification and fraud handling match Paylane’s operating requirements.
